Ice Hockey

Hockey is a fast-paced team sport played on ice, where two teams use sticks to shoot a puck into the opponent’s net and score goals. Known for its speed, skill, and excitement, hockey combines teamwork, strategy, and athleticism in a thrilling game enjoyed around the world.
